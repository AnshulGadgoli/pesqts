Chapter XII

Clubs of RRC

"Explore the automotive teams, innovation labs, performing arts, and debating circles at the Ring Road Campus."

CAMPUS LIFE
RRC CLUB DIRECTORY

PES Innovation Lab

Research

The university's premier student-run incubation and software engineering lab (formerly Microsoft Innovation Lab). Its alumni network spans over 15 years, reaching top global organizations.

Flagship: HackerSpace

IEEE RAS PESU

Robotics

IEEE Robotics and Automation Society focuses on robotics, embedded systems, automation, and intelligent machines through hands-on workshops and real-world projects.

IEEE CS PESU

Coding

IEEE Computer Society is dedicated to software development, programming, artificial intelligence, and hosting coding contests, workshops, and hackathons.

IEEE APS

ECE

IEEE Antennas and Propagation Society focuses on antennas, wireless communication, RF engineering, and electromagnetics, catering especially to Electronics students.

IEEE CTSoc

Hardware

IEEE Consumer Technology Society is focused on smart consumer tech, hardware design, embedded systems, and product prototyping projects.

Alcoding Club

Competitive

Dedicated to advanced competitive programming, data structures, and algorithms. Hosts coding contests and trains students for major programming events.

Q⟩Forest

Quantum

Quantum Computing Community of PESU. Fosters peer learning in quantum physics, quantum gates, quantum algorithms, and future computation architectures.

Equinox

Space

The Space Club of PESU. Explores aeromodelling, rocket engineering, astronomy, satellite telemetry systems, and astrophysics.

SahayAI

Social AI

Uses artificial intelligence, machine learning models, and software tools to build applications that assist the elderly and those in need.

Nexus

AIML

The department club for Computer Science & Engineering. Explores deep learning, neural networks, computer vision, and machine learning models.

AURA Club

AIML

Department club for CSE (AI & ML). Focuses on algorithmic data analysis, predictive modeling, and AI ethics discussions.

Encode.AI

AIML

Technical AIML domain club. Organizes AI-themed hackathons, workshops on natural language processing, and neural network projects.

Arch Club

AIML

Department club for CSE (AI & ML), studying software engineering workflows, model architectures, and data warehousing.

GronIT

Green Tech

Green Technology Club of the CSE Department. Focuses on green software engineering, energy-efficient algorithms, and hardware recycling.

Embrione

CSE

The official Computer Science & Engineering department club. Conducts technical inductions, bootcamps, and coding contests.

PesOS

Operating Sys

Focuses on operating systems, kernel modification, hardware virtualization, security hardening, and open-source contributions.

Vega Racing Electric

Racing

The electric racing car engineering team. Designs, prototypes, and builds high-performance electric Formula student racecars.

Team Haya

Racing

The premier racing club under the Mechanical Department, engineering Formula combustion cars for international FSAE events.

Team Haya Off-Roading

Racing

Focuses on rugged off-road automotive designs. Engineers All-Terrain Vehicles (ATVs) for BAJA SAE competitions.

SILICON

ECE NiCE

ECE department vertical for Next Generation Integrated Circuits and Electronics (NiCE). Focuses on VLSI design and microelectronics.

COMNET

ECE WiNC

ECE department vertical for Wireless Networking and Communication (WiNC). Explores wireless grids, RF routing, and LTE/5G protocols.

SPIRAL

ECE SpAsE

ECE department vertical for Signal Processing and Systems Engineering (SpAsE). Studies digital filters, audio/video analysis, and systems control.

JOEL

ECE Lab

The Joy of Engineering Lab. A dynamic ECE sandbox encouraging hobbyist projects, sensor assemblies, and hardware hacking.

init

BCA

The official department club of Computer Applications (BCA), hosting technical events, web design competitions, and database seminars.

GCube PESU

Gaming

The official gaming club of PES RR Campus. Coordinates esports tournaments, console game trials, and local LAN events.

Center for Info Security

Research Center

PES University Center for Information Security, Forensics, and Cyber Resilience. Focuses on networking security and cryptography.

CORI

Research Center

Crucible of Research and Innovation (CORI). Coordinates university nanosatellite designs, space communication links, and research projects.

CDSAML–CCBD

Research Center

Center for Data Science & Applied Machine Learning / Center for Cloud Computing & Big Data. Undertakes cloud scaling research.

Center for Robotics

Research Center

Center for Robotics, Automation, and Intelligent Systems. Researches autonomous pathing, drone kinetics, and computer-guided actuators.

cHEAL

Research Center

Centre for Healthcare Engineering and Learning. Integrates hardware sensor design with machine learning tools to solve diagnostics.

C-HACS

Research Center

Centre for Hardware Acceleration and Chip Security. Focuses on testing chip architectures and VLSI layout evaluations.

C2S Lab

Research Center

Chip to Startup Lab. An initiative supported by MeiTY introducing undergraduates to industrial VLSI designs and chip fabrication.

Angika

Classical Dance

Celebrating the heritage of classical Indian dance forms, performing fusions at major university cultural events.

Antara

Bollywood Dance

The high-energy Bollywood dance crew of PES University. Performs routines at major college fests and cultural gatherings.

Team Encore

Western Dance

Official Western Dance Crew of PES University RR Campus. Represents the college in national street dance and hip-hop events.

Team Sanskrithi

Contemporary Dance

Indian Contemporary Dance Team, merging modern choreographies, classical expressions, and artistic dance themes.

Team Trance

Western Dance

The western dance crew representing the university in national college events and showcase competitions.

Pitch Please

Acapella

The acapella and acoustic music club, focused on vocal harmonies, beatboxing, and acoustic guitar arrangements.

The Music Club

Bands & Acoustic

Official Music Club of PES University RR Campus, coordinating student bands and organizing regular musical jams.

Ninaada

Indian Music

Focuses on Indian classical and semi-classical music, combining acoustic instruments with traditional melodies.

Nautanki

Theatre

The drama and theatre club. Stages annual college plays, street theatre (Nukkad Natak), acting classes, and scripting workshops.

Kalaangana

BCA Cultural

Official Cultural and Performing Arts Club of the BCA Department, organizing dance, music, and art events.

PESU Meraki

Fashion

The official fashion club of PES University. Focuses on creative styling, modeling, design concepts, and runways.

Linguista

Languages

The language club celebrating foreign and local languages through translation projects, grammar circles, and dialogue games.

Jnaanada PESU

Linguistics

Techno-Linguistic Club exploring the intersection of programming structures, computational linguistics, and literature.

The Grimm Readers

Book Club

The book club hosting regular reading circles, book discussions, author reviews, and literature exchanges.

Quotient Quiz Club

Trivia

The premier quiz society of RRC. Hosts weekly general quizzes, sports trivia, business quizzes, and national quiz bowls.

Kannada Koota

Techno-Cultural

The official Kannada cultural club of RR Campus, organizing activities and panels that highlight local heritage.

HoPES PESU

Videography

Official videography club of RR Campus, training students in editing, cinematography, sound, and recording fests.

PIXELS Photography

Photography

Official photography club of RR Campus, documenting student life, campus architecture, and college festivals.

PESU Talkies

Filmmaking

Official filmmaking club of the university, conducting workshops on scriptwriting, direction, and film editing.

PESU Podcast

Media

Produces regular podcast episodes featuring academic guests, industry professionals, and student club heads.

Mosaic PESU

Fine Arts

The official fine arts and painting club, organizing workshops on sketching, canvas art, and college murals.

DSGNR

Design

Focuses on UI/UX, graphic design, layout engineering, typography, and professional brand designs.

PES MUN Society (MUNSOC)

Diplomacy

The Model United Nations society, active in simulating UN conferences, global diplomacy debates, and drafting resolutions.

Debating Society (DEBSOC)

Debate

The official debating club, training students in parliamentary debate formats, public speaking, and policy arguments.

TEDxPESU

TED Talks

Fosters sharing of ideas by organizing annual TEDx talks featuring guest speakers from diverse fields.

Changemakers' Society

Social Impact

Focused on environmental cleanups, civic campaigns, sustainability drives, and social responsibility projects.

Collegiate Social Responsibility (CSR)

Social Service

CSR is dedicated to creating a positive social impact through community-driven initiatives. The club organizes the Community Service Bootstrap for the Engineering students, Blood Donation Camps, and Self-Defence Workshops and more, encouraging students to contribute meaningfully beyond the classroom.

Aikya PESU

Community

Focused on social service campaigns, teaching programs at local schools, and community engagement initiatives. Aikya and CSR organize volunteering support together, working on programs like the Community Service Bootstrap to help students contribute beyond the classroom.

Rotaract Club

Social Service

A student wing of Rotary International. Engages students in blood donation drives, school teaching, and local service.

Entrepreneurship Cell

E-Cell

Fosters student-led startup ideas, entrepreneurship workshops, hackathons, and incubation pitches.

CIE

Incubation

Center of Innovation and Entrepreneurship. The official college incubation center assisting student startups.

PESU Venture Labs

Funding

The official startup funding accelerator of the university, supporting student teams with venture capital support.

Enactus PESU

Social Biz

Combines business metrics with social welfare, developing startup solutions to resolve public issues.

The Trading Hub

Finance

A student group exploring financial literacy, market analysis, stock trading, and portfolio management.

Beetles Business Club

Business

Encourages skill-building in business case studies, consulting simulations, and mock trade markets.

IRA PESU

UGC Service

The official student welfare service club, organizing awareness drives and campaigns under UGC guidelines.
