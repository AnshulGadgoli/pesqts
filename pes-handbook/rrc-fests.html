Chapter XIII

Fests of RRC

"A chronicle of the massive technical conferences, cultural showcases, and annual celebrations at Ring Road."

CAMPUS VIBES
RRC FESTIVAL GUIDE

Life at PES University isn't just about classes, assignments, and exams. Throughout the academic year, both the Ring Road and Electronic City campuses host a wide variety of events that cater to almost every interest.

From technical workshops, coding competitions, hackathons, quizzes, business conclaves, startup summits, TEDx talks, cultural performances, sports tournaments, and networking sessions to fun student-led activities, there's always something happening on campus. Whether you're looking to learn a new skill, compete, build your resume, or simply have fun, you'll never run out of opportunities.

While dozens of events take place every semester, here are some of the flagship events that every PESU student should know about.

FLAGSHIP FEST
AATMATRISHA

🎉 Aatmatrisha (AT)

PES University's Flagship Techno-Cultural Festival

If there's one event that every PESU student eagerly looks forward to, it's Aatmatrisha, more commonly known as AT. It is the university's largest and most prestigious techno-cultural festival, held annually at the Ring Road Campus, and attracts participants from colleges across the country.

For two exciting days, the campus transforms into a hub of competitions, performances, concerts, exhibitions, workshops, and celebrations. Whether you're interested in technology, business, music, dance, gaming, photography, or simply looking to have fun, there's something for everyone.

🌟 What Makes AT So Special?

Aatmatrisha is much more than just a college fest- it's an experience. The festival brings together students from various disciplines. Almost every student club in PESU contributes by organizing one or more events, making it one of the busiest and most vibrant weekends of the year.

🎤 Pro Nite

The highlight of every Aatmatrisha is undoubtedly Pro Nite. Every year, nationally renowned singers, bands, and performers take the stage in front of thousands of students. It is one of the most anticipated events of the academic year and often becomes the talk of the campus for weeks.

😂 Comedy Night

Comedy Night has become another crowd favourite in recent years. Popular stand-up comedians perform live on campus, drawing huge audiences and often selling out within hours of ticket sales opening.

🎭 Cul Nite

Cultural Night is where the talent of PES University truly shines. Student clubs spend months preparing performances ranging from dance and music to theatre, fashion shows, and beatboxing. It's one of the best opportunities to witness the incredible creativity and talent within the university.

👕 Dress-Up Days

The excitement of Aatmatrisha isn't limited to just the competitions and concerts, the celebrations begin well before the main fest. Throughout AT Week, each day follows a different dress-up theme, with students dressing according to the announced theme. It's one of the few times during the semester when you'll see almost the entire campus enthusiastically participating, making for an incredibly lively atmosphere.

As the week progresses, the campus transforms with:

  • 🎨 Decorative installations
  • 📸 Photo booths
  • ✨ Creative pop-up displays
  • 🏛️ Themed decorations across campus
  • 🎭 Students dressed in vibrant outfits matching the day's theme

Ethnic Day: The highlight of the week is undoubtedly Ethnic Day. On this day, students arrive dressed in their finest traditional attires: sarees, kurtas, sherwanis, lehengas, and many more beautiful outfits. The campus is buzzing with energy as friends gather to click photos, department groups organize photoshoots, and almost every corner of the campus turns into a backdrop for memories. If there's one day you'll want to have your phone fully charged and your camera ready, it's Ethnic Day!

📅 How AT Week Unfolds

🌸 Day 1 - Ethnic Day & Cultural Night (Friday)

The first day of the festival is celebrated as Ethnic Day, where students come dressed in traditional attire. During the day, most competitions continue as usual, while the evening features Cul Nite, showcasing performances by the university's dance teams, music clubs, theatre groups, fashion teams, and many other student organizations. It's one of the most vibrant evenings of the entire festival.

🎤 Day 2 – Comedy Night & Pro Nite (Saturday)

The second day is primarily dedicated to the headline entertainment events. Unlike the first day, very few competitions are scheduled, allowing students to focus on enjoying the performances. The evening typically features Comedy Night with a popular stand-up comedian, and Pro Nite where renowned singers, bands, or artists perform live. For most students, this is the grand finale of Aatmatrisha and the moment they've been waiting for all week.

💡 Senior Tip: Ethnic Day photos

Ethnic Day is one of the most photographed days of the academic year. Coordinate outfits with your friends beforehand, reach campus early to avoid crowds at popular photo spots, and make the most of the decorated campus, you'll probably look back at those pictures years later!

⚠️ A Reality Check

  • 🎟️ Ticket Sales: Tickets for Comedy Night and Pro Nite are in extremely high demand. Prices can be on the higher side, especially for premium categories. Ticket sales often experience heavy rushes; website slowdowns, payment failures, delayed confirmations, and other technical glitches are not uncommon due to the sheer number of students trying to book simultaneously. Offline ticket queues can stretch for several hours, and preferred categories often sell out fast. Purchase tickets as early as possible!
  • 🚶 Entry to the Venue: Once you have your ticket, be prepared for another queue. For headline events like Comedy Night and Pro Nite, entry queues can sometimes extend to 4–5 hours, depending on the crowd and security arrangements. Arriving well in advance can save you from missing a part of the performance.
  • 🎒 Things You Can Carry: Security during the event is generally very strict. You'll usually be asked not to carry backpacks, water bottles, or other loose belongings into the venue. It's best to carry only your College ID, Phone, Wallet, Keys, and a very small pouch (if permitted). Anything beyond the essentials may have to be left outside or may not be allowed inside. Food and beverages are available inside the venue, although they're priced higher than usual.
  • 🎫 VIP vs Regular Tickets: AT generally offers multiple ticket categories, such as Regular and VIP. While VIP tickets may include certain benefits depending on the event, many students feel that the difference in experience between the ticket categories isn't always as significant as expected. Consider your budget and expectations before choosing a ticket category.

🤝 Organizing Aatmatrisha

One of the most impressive aspects of the festival is that it is almost entirely student-run. Planning for the festival begins several months in advance, making it one of the largest student-led initiatives at PES University. Working on the organizing team can help you gain experience in Event Management, Leadership, Logistics, Marketing, Sponsorship, Public Relations, Stage 'N Infra, and Networking. However, organizing the festival requires a significant commitment of time and effort throughout the semester.

Many students have also expressed concerns over the years regarding aspects of the organizing experience, including:

  • The requirement to purchase the official volunteer kit, which typically includes the AT-branded T-shirt.
  • Selection for certain teams or leadership roles being highly competitive, with some students feeling that the process could be more transparent.
  • The workload during the weeks leading up to the festival, which can be difficult to balance alongside academics.

These experiences vary from person to person, and while many volunteers thoroughly enjoy the experience, others feel it wasn't the right fit for them.

💡 Senior Tip: Volunteer vs Participant

If your goal is to develop skills such as leadership, event management, teamwork, sponsorship, public speaking, or networking, Aatmatrisha isn't your only opportunity. PES University has dozens of active clubs that organize events throughout the year, from workshops and hackathons to conferences, quizzes, summits, and community initiatives. Being part of these clubs can provide the same skills, often with more consistent involvement and opportunities to grow into leadership positions over multiple semesters. Because of this, many seniors recommend enjoying your first Aatmatrisha as a participant rather than an organizer. Take part in competitions, attend the concerts, explore the events, cheer for your friends, and experience the atmosphere. Once you've seen how the festival works from the outside, you'll be in a much better position to decide whether organizing future editions is something you'd genuinely enjoy.

COMMUNITY CAUSE
SAMARPANA

Samarpana

A Tribute to the Indian Armed Forces

Unlike most college events that last for a weekend, Samarpana is a long-running initiative spanning 2–3 months. Organized as a tribute to the Indian Armed Forces, it combines fundraising, community service, and student engagement, culminating in one of the university's largest annual events.

The journey begins with the official launch of Samarpana, marking the start of a series of fundraising and awareness activities organized by students across the campus. Throughout the following weeks, numerous fundraising initiatives are conducted, including bake sales, merchandise stalls, donation drives, and other student-led activities. Every contribution goes towards supporting the families of armed forces personnel.

💃 Garba Night

Held over two evenings, it brings together hundreds of students dressed in vibrant traditional attire for an unforgettable celebration of music, dance, and festivities. The event has become synonymous with endless photo sessions, energetic Garba and Dandiya dances, and one of the most lively social gatherings of the odd semester.

🏃 Run with the Jawans

The flagship event of Samarpana is Run with the Jawans, a marathon that symbolizes solidarity with India's armed forces. The event features both 5 km and 10 km categories and witnesses participation from students, faculty, alumni, defence personnel, and the general public. Months of planning by the student organizing team go into making the marathon a success.

🤝 Felicitation Ceremony

Samarpana concludes with a heartfelt felicitation ceremony honouring the families of armed forces personnel. Through the funds raised during the campaign, PES University extends support in the form of financial assistance, free legal aid, and medical aid, making Samarpana much more than just another college event.

💡 Why Samarpana Stands Out

While most college fests focus on entertainment and competitions, Samarpana is driven by a larger purpose. It gives students an opportunity to contribute to a meaningful cause while participating in memorable events like Garba Night and the marathon, making it one of the most unique traditions at PES University.

PERFORMING ARTS
CHORDS FEST

🎶 Chords

The Indian Music Festival

Chords is PES University's flagship Indian music festival, celebrating the richness and diversity of Indian music. Every year, it brings together talented musicians from PESU and colleges across the country, making it one of the most prestigious music competitions on campus.

The festival serves as a platform for aspiring singers, instrumentalists, bands, and classical performers to showcase their talent before a live audience. Students perform across multiple categories, representing a wide range of Indian musical traditions and contemporary styles. Throughout the event, you'll witness performances ranging from Hindustani and Carnatic classical music to Bollywood, folk, fusion, semi-classical, and contemporary Indian music. Instrumental performances, solo vocals, duets, and band competitions ensure that there is something for every music enthusiast.

💡 Senior Tip: Attend and Secure Tickets Early

Even if you're not participating, make time to attend Chords. The performances are of an exceptionally high standard, and it's one of the best opportunities to experience live Indian music on campus while supporting your fellow students.

And most importantly, don't wait until the last minute to collect your ticket. Even though Chords is free to attend, the number of seats is limited, and tickets tend to run out quickly. Keep an eye out for announcements regarding the ticket distribution counters and grab yours as soon as they're available.

SCIENCE & MGMT
TAMS

🔬 TAMS

The Amateur Scientist & Manager

TAMS (The Amateur Scientist & Manager) is one of PES University's oldest and most prestigious student competitions. Participants compete in a series of technical, scientific, managerial, and business-oriented challenges that test innovation, analytical thinking, teamwork, and problem-solving skills.
