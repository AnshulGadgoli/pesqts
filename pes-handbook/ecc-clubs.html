Chapter XV

Clubs of ECC

"Explore the technical projects, dance troupes, musical circles, and department clubs at the Electronic City Campus."

CAMPUS LIFE
ECC CLUB DIRECTORY

ACM

Coding

The Association for Computing Machinery Student Chapter. Conducts mentorship circles, developer bootcamps, and high-intensity coding competitions.

Flagship Event: HackNight

CodeChef

Competitive

Promotes competitive coding through regular algorithms practice, data structures bootcamps, and inter-collegiate programming contest preparation.

Flagship Event: LeetCode101

Equinox

Aerospace

Provides a launchpad for astronomy and space sciences, conducting research initiatives in aerospace engineering and orbital satellite innovation.

Flagship Event: Stellar Discoveries

HSP

Systems

Heap Stack Pointer. Specializes in low-level operating systems, kernel architecture, open-source documentation, and command line tooling seminars.

Flagship Event: Tilde

IEEE Student Branch

Research

Maintains research-driven student initiatives covering hardware engineering, telecommunications, networking architectures, and humanitarian designs.

Flagship Event: Kalpana - Hack for Humanity

Layer8

Security

A specialized student-led cybersecurity network hosting hands-on labs in penetration testing, network defense systems, and ethical hacking.

Flagship Event: Capture The Flag (CTF)

Neural Hive

AI / ML

Bridges data engineering with AI/ML systems. Focuses on neural networks, computer vision, data analysis models, and expert speaker panels.

Flagship Event: AI/ML Hackathons

Parallax

Graphics

Focuses on graphics programming, gaming mechanics, Unity/Unreal engines, virtual reality (VR) models, and interactive software designs.

Flagship Event: Game Dev Workshops

Silicon

Hardware

Builds hardware-oriented prototypes. Focuses on IoT setups, microcontrollers, embedded circuits, and physical computing architectures.

Flagship Event: Hardware Projects

Team Avions

Aviation

Aero-design team focusing on building RC planes, drone mechanics, aeromodelling, flight simulators, and aerodynamics studies.

Flagship Event: Aeromodelling Projects

Team Vegavath

Automotive

Automotive research group constructing high-efficiency racing vehicle projects, electric vehicle layouts, and machine robotics parts.

Flagship Event: Racing Vehicle Projects

Nexus AWS

Cloud

A collegiate partnership with the AWS User Group Madurai. Explores modern cloud computing principles, serverless hosting structures, and data security architectures through workshops and monthly Skill Sprints.

Flagship Event: Nimbus 2000 (Cloud CTF Hackathon)

Nirantara

Dance

Specializes in Indian classical and Indo-contemporary dance styles, performing showcases at college fests and national championships.

Flagship Event: Classical Dance Performances

Terpsichore

Dance

Focuses on energetic Bollywood, western freestyle, hip-hop, and Indian fusion dance routines in inter-university competitions.

Flagship Event: Dance Competitions

Swarantraka

Music

Promotes classical and semi-classical Indian music through vocal ensembles, acoustic instrumentation, and traditional fusion sessions.

Flagship Event: Music Performances

Tonic and a IX

Music

Hosts rock, pop, jazz, and western live bands on campus. Organizes jam sessions, band line-ups, and music workshops.

Flagship Event: Band Performances

Clefhanger

Music

The acapella and acoustic music club of the campus. Focuses on vocal harmony arrangements, unplugged jam sessions, and acoustic collaborations.

Flagship Event: Acoustic Jams & Acapella Showcases

Mangobites

Drama

The university drama society. Focuses on street plays (Nukkad Natak), scriptwriting, stage acting workshops, and short film productions.

Flagship Event: Street Plays & Short Films

Media Club

Media

Encourages creative photography, video journalism, post-production editing, motion design, and digital content coverage of campus events.

Flagship Event: Photography Exhibitions

Design Club

Arts

Nurtures visual creativity through graphic design workshops, UI/UX bootcamps, and creative digital art challenges.

Flagship Event: CS Design Bootstrap

Avyakta - Ira

Cultural

Celebrating heritage and creative expression. Ira organizes cultural showcases, fine art exhibitions, and traditional storytelling programs.

Flagship Event: Cultural Showcases & Exhibitions

Panache

Fashion

The official fashion club. Specializes in costume design, ramp walks, apparel themes, and styling concepts.

Flagship Event: Flagship Fashion Show (during Maaya)

Apostrophe

Literature

Specializes in debating, poetry circles, creative writing sessions, open mic platforms, and book discussion circles.

Flagship Event: Open Mics

MUNSoc

Diplomacy

The Model United Nations Society. Engages students in public speaking, geopolitics debates, international relations, and resolution drafting.

Flagship Event: Model UN Conferences

QQC

Trivia

The Quotient Quiz Club. Organizes weekly trivia rounds, general knowledge contests, and represents the campus in national quiz bowls.

Flagship Event: Trivia Challenges

E-Club

Business

The Entrepreneurship Club. Fosters business ideation, startup bootcamps, financial strategy seminars, and student product validation.

Flagship Event: Pitch-Offs

Rotaract

Service

Rotary-affiliated youth chapter. Inspires student leadership through local volunteering, community donation campaigns, and social welfare drives.

Flagship Event: Community Service Projects

Socioholics

Pharmacy

The pioneer student club of the Pharmacy Department. Serves as a multidisciplinary platform bridging healthcare with other sectors like business, technology, and global policy.

Flagship Event: Healthcare Integration Seminars
