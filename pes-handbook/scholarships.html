Chapter IX

Scholarships & Merit Awards

"Rewarding academic excellence, diligent efforts, and intellectual achievements across all campuses."

ACADEMIC INCENTIVES
MRD SCHOLARSHIPS • CNR AWARDS • DISTINCTIONS

PES University actively encourages academic excellence by offering generous tuition fee refunds and merit incentives. Scholarships are calculated and awarded on a regular semester/annual basis, allowing top-performing students to significantly offset their education expenses.

Top 5% of Batch

1. MRD Scholarship

50% tuition fee refund is awarded along with an official merit certificate. This premier academic honor is evaluated and given based on a student’s Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA).

Next 20% of Batch

2. CNR Scholarship

40% tuition fee refund is awarded for the next segment of the batch (ranks 6% – 25%) along with a merit certificate. This scholarship is evaluated and given based on a student's Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA).

SGPA 7.75+ Cutoff

3. Distinction Award

An official merit certificate is awarded to all students who secure an SGPA of 7.75 or above in a semester, along with a cash prize incentive of ₹ 2,000.

Rules & Eligibility Guidelines

• Duration Limit: Scholarships are awarded exclusively for the first 3 years of your study program.

• Mutual Exclusivity: Students are eligible to receive only one award out of the three options in any given semester.

• CNR Minimum Cutoff: The absolute minimum cutoff score required for the CNR Scholarship is 8.50 SGPA. If a student's rank falls within the top 25% (6% – 25%) but their SGPA is below 8.50, the scholarship will not be granted.

• Campus & Department Allocations: Scholarships are processed separately for both campuses and are separated by program branch.

• First Year Unity: In the first year, all B.Tech branches are grouped together under the Science & Humanities (S&H) department. Ranks are computed across the entire department, meaning there is no branch separation (e.g., CSE, ECE) for first-year scholarship eligibility.

• Fee Quotas: The scholarship value remains constant at 50% or 40% of your respective tuition fee, regardless of whether you secured admission via JEE, KCET, or the Management quota.
