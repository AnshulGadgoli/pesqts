Introduction

Congratulations, and welcome to the PES University family! 🎉 Starting college is an exciting milestone, and with it comes plenty of questions - where to go, who to contact, what to expect, and how to make the most of your time here. That's exactly what this handbook is here for.

Think of this as your friendly guide to life at PESU. Whether you're looking for campus information, university resources, or tips to navigate your first few weeks, we've got you covered. This handbook is a living document, so we'll continue adding new information, useful resources, and handy tips to make your PESU journey even smoother.

Here's to an unforgettable four years filled with learning, friendships, and memories!

Our Campuses

Ring Road (RR) Campus

The Ring Road Campus is the university's flagship campus and home to the highest number of academic departments. It features outstanding infrastructure, research blocks, sports setups, and campus facilities.

📍 Outer Ring Road, Banashankari, Bengaluru

Electronic City (EC) Campus

Located in the heart of Electronic City, this campus offers a spacious and modern learning environment. It is rapidly expanding, with a teaching hospital and medical college, making it an exciting hub for future growth.

Hanumanth Nagar (HN) Campus
