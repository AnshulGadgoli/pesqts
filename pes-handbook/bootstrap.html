Welcome to Bootstrap

Bootstrap is a 5-day orientation program conducted before regular classes begin. It is organized by 4 to 5 student clubs with support from first-year professors and senior volunteers. The program runs from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM and is one of the best opportunities to meet your batchmates before academics begin. No prior knowledge is required. Seniors and faculty will guide you throughout every activity, so just come with an open mind and enjoy the experience.

Eligibility: Students who have obtained admission via PESU JEE Mains Counselling, PESU KCET Counselling, higher fee quota and those who enter via KEA R1 counselling can take part. Those who join through R2 and R3 would not be able to take part due to the delayed counselling process. However, if the process does get over quickly or earlier, they will also be able to join in the bootstrap activities.

Colour Groups

The entire batch is divided into five colour groups: Blue, Yellow, Green, Red, Purple. Before Bootstrap begins, you'll receive an email mentioning your colour group, which remains the same throughout the five days. Within each colour group, you'll also be assigned a sub-group that changes daily, allowing you to work with different people.

Purpose of Bootstrap

  • ✔ Network with your batchmates and make new friends
  • ✔ Improve communication and teamwork
  • ✔ Work with random teammates to solve problems
  • ✔ Step out of your comfort zone
  • This is widely considered one of the most memorable and enjoyable weeks of first year, so don't miss it!

Important Points

  • • The order of events is different for every colour group. While your friends may attend different events on different days, everyone gets to experience all events by the end.
  • • No prior knowledge is required for any event. Seniors and faculty will guide and support you throughout, and no one will judge you for not knowing something.
  • • The biggest takeaway from Bootstrap isn't the events themselves. It's the people you meet and the friendships you build.

EC Campus Events

Event 1. Electrical & Electronics

A basic introduction to electronics through hands-on activities. Work with wires, breadboards, LEDs, resistors, and other basic components to build simple circuits under the guidance of seniors and faculty.

Event 2. Biotechnology

Conducted by students and faculty from the RR Campus. Learn about plants, biotechnology, and real-world applications through demonstrations, scientific hacks, and fun facts.

Event 3. Mechanical & Team Avions

Hosted by Team VegaVath: learn automobile maintenance (changing tyres, replacing engine oil) and view their custom-built Racing Cars. Interact with Team Avions (aviation club) to see drone and aircraft demos.

Event 4. Community Development

Divided into groups to paint government schools, visit villages or old-age homes, and help cut vegetables at biocentres. Materials and lunch are provided by the college. Carry your PES T-shirt, hat, and sunscreen.

Event 5. Design Thinking

Join your sub-group, pick a domain, and pitch solutions to a rotating customer. DJ, music, mini-games. The winning team receives a box of Ferrero Rocher chocolates!

RR Campus Events

Departments involved: CSE, AIML, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Biotechnology, Community Service. Mechanical and biotech run on the same day (separated by lunch). Since there are 6 sets of activities and 5 days, 1 color misses 1 activity.

* Note: There are usually multiple events happening on a single day, so the exact schedule will be sent to you.

Event 1. Electrical & Electronics

A basic introduction to electronics through hands-on activities. Seniors and faculty guide you through lab visits and basic circuit work.

Event 2. Biotechnology

Learn about plants and biotech applications via interactive demos, simple scientific hacks, and biology experiments.

Event 3. Mechanical

Hosted by the mechanical department and racing clubs. Learn tyre and engine oil changes for both 2 and 4 wheelers under guidance, and see Racing Cars built by students.

Event 4. Community Development (No Phone Day)

Visit villages, paint schools, or cut logs, weed greenhouses, and repot seedlings at biocentres. Strictly a no phone day: leave phones in your bag or van. Materials and lunch provided. Carry PES T-shirt, hat, sunscreen, water bottle.

Event 5. Design Thinking

Brainstorm concept pitches on chart papers for a rotating customer. Winning team receives Ferrero Rocher chocolates.

Event 6. ECE Labs Tour

Tour labs including signal processing, VLSI, wireless, quantum, and the JOEL (Joy of Engineering Lab) for robotics and embedded systems projects. You'll be introduced via different activities like experiments or running simple codes and visiting the antenna labs.

Event 7. AIML

Fun demos, neural network visualizations, and interactive sessions with the machine learning clubs.

What to Carry Checklist

  • College ID Card (temporary passcode paper or app profile is fine)
  • Water Bottle (essential for outdoor days)
  • Lunch or cash for cafeteria meals (except Community Development day)
  • PES T-Shirt (required for Community Development day)
  • Hat / Cap and Sunscreen (for school painting or biocentres)

Most importantly, bring an excited mindset and a willingness to meet new people. Leave your shyness and introversion at home!
