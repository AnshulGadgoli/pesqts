Chapter VII

SRN & PRN Decoded

"Numeric identifiers serving as the structural anchor of your academic record."
REGISTRATION DATABASE
PRN ANALYSIS • SRN BREAKDOWN

Every student admitted to PES University is assigned two unique identification numbers - the PRN (Personal Registration Number) and the SRN (Student Registration Number). While both are important, you'll find yourself using your SRN much more frequently during your time at PESU.

Permanent Registration (PRN)

PRN Anatomy (Click segments to decode)

PES 1 2026 001234

University Prefix

The PRN is assigned at the time of admission and serves as your initial identification number until your SRN is generated. It always begins with the official institutional prefix "PES".


Campus Division Code

Represents the campus of admission:
1 = Ring Road (RR) Campus
2 = Electronic City (EC) Campus


Admission Year Registry

The full 4-digit calendar year during which your admission was officially processed by the administration (e.g. 2026).


Unique Serial Sequence

A unique 5 or 6 digit sequential roll code assigned systematically to catalog your records in the administrative database.

Student Registration (SRN)

SRN Anatomy (Click segments to decode)

PES 1 UG 26 CS 999

University prefix

The SRN is your official student identity number, issued a few weeks after classes commence. Once allotted, it becomes your primary identifier for logging into the Academy Portal, accessing the App, registering for courses, exams, and assignments.


Campus Division Code

Identifies your academic campus:
1 = Ring Road (RR) Campus
2 = Electronic City (EC) Campus


Academic Level

Defines the degree level category: UG for Undergraduate programmes, and PG for Postgraduate programmes.


Year of Enrolment

The last two digits of your year of admission (e.g. 26 represents a student admitted in 2026).


Academic Branch Code

Represents your program department:
CS = Computer Science • AM = CS (AI & ML)
EC = Electronics & Comm. • BT = Biotechnology
ME = Mechanical • BD = Design
BA = BCA • BB = BBA • BL = BBA LLB
BC = B.Com • PY = Psychology


Unique Roll Sequence

A unique 3-digit student roll index sequence within your branch (e.g., 999).

💡 Tip: Memorize your SRN as early as possible

You'll be asked for it frequently - whether it's for examinations, assignments, lab records, club registrations, or interacting with university portals. It will be printed on your ID card, but it is best to memorize it.
