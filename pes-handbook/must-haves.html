Chapter III

The Must-Haves

"Equip yourself with the tools of critical inquiry and analytical precision."

MATERIAL CHECK
REQUIRED STATIONERY • TECHNICAL SPEC SHEET
SPECIFICATION LOG
NOTEBOOKS

Lecture Notebooks

A simple notebook is all you need to get started. At the beginning of the 1st semester, the university usually provides a 5-subject spiral notebook, which is enough for rough work or practice.

Recommendations

  • Get a larger 5-subject notebook or maintain individual books per subject for ease of understanding.
  • Mathematics recommendation: Maintain a separate notebook as you'll be doing a lot of problems.
SUBMISSION MANUAL
BLUE BOOK

Assignment Journals

Most courses require assignments to be written in a blue book (typically priced around ₹15–20). These are readily available at the campus bookstore/Campus Mart.

Shopping Tip

  • A cheaper choice is to get blue books from outside of campus where they are available for lesser prices.
  • If you are in a hurry, Campus Mart is always available.
HARDWARE MATRIX
fx-991CW

Scientific Calculator

A non-programmable scientific calculator is an essential tool for engineering students and will be used frequently throughout your coursework. Programmable calculators are prohibited during exams.

Authorized Models

  • Casio fx-991CW (Preferred choice, constants and more features built-in)
  • Casio fx-991ES Plus
  • Casio fx-82MS
  • Tip: Seniors sell calculators in working condition at reduced, negotiable prices.
