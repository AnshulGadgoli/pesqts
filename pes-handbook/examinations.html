Chapter IV

Examinations

"Assessment is the mirror of true understanding and academic integrity."

ISA & ESA TIMELINE • DOUBLE EVALUATION AUDIT

Examinations at PES University are divided into two components: In-Semester Assessments (ISAs) and End Semester Assessments (ESAs). Together, they determine your final grade for each course. Attending classes regularly, completing assignments on time, and staying consistent will make exam season much less stressful.

In-Semester Assessments (ISAs)

ISAs are conducted twice every semester and evaluate understanding as the course progresses. Together, the ISAs contribute 50% of your final course grade.

  • Duration: 1 hour 5 minutes. Conducted for 40 marks.
  • Portions: 1st ISA covers Unit 1 & 2; 2nd ISA covers Unit 3 & 4.
  • Venue: University examination centres (The JAGS Nest in RR Campus, Mechanical Block in EC Campus).
  • Exam Pattern: Completely digital question paper displayed on computer screen. MCQs: 16 questions x 1 mark, 4 questions x 2 marks. Descriptive: 4 questions x 4 marks (2 per unit).
  • Note: No negative marking for MCQs.
Phase I (ISAs)

Occurs twice dynamically in a semester timeline.

End Semester Assessments (ESAs)

ESAs are the comprehensive examinations conducted at the end of each semester. They contribute 50% of the final course grade.

  • Duration: 3 hours. Conducted for 100 marks.
  • Portions: Comprehensive coverage of all 4 units.
  • Question Type: Entirely descriptive. The paper is displayed digitally on screens, while answers are written in the provided booklet (or typed/draw combo).
Phase II (ESAs)

Final comprehensive examination at the terminal week.

The Answer Booklet

Answers are written in standard booklets immediately digitized after exams (20 pages for ISAs, 40 pages for ESAs; includes graphs/rough sheets, no extra booklets provided).

OMR Sheet Guidelines: Fill SRN, Name in CAPITAL letters, Signature, Subject Code, Subject Name (Full, e.g. "Engineering Chemistry"), and Slot. You must bubble the question number on every page to facilitate scanning.

Before the Examination

The examination timetable is usually released about a week in advance.

Seating Arrangement: Seating assignments change for every exam and are released on the night before or morning of the exam via the PESU App or PESU Academy Portal.

SEATING CODE

Admittance seating maps available on mobile portal.

Question Paper Preparation

One of the unique aspects of PESU examinations is that the final question paper is generated only on the day of the examination.

The Anchor Faculty and Co-Anchor Faculty each prepare a separate question paper. The university's software automatically selects portions from both papers just before the exam, making leaks virtually impossible.

DYNAMIC ALGORITHM

Algorithmic compilation of dual question pools.

How Papers Are Evaluated

Every answer booklet is scanned and digitized. The evaluation process is entirely digital.

Digital Evaluation: Examiners do not receive an entire script. Instead, the software assigns one specific question to a faculty member, who evaluates that single question for every student in the course to ensure consistency.

Marking Key: Evaluators follow an official key including essential concepts, keywords, and derivations. Writing in your own words is acceptable if technical keywords are present.

DIGITAL MARKING

Distributed question-wise digital evaluation.

AI-Assisted Evaluation Policy

To improve consistency during evaluation, PES University employs an AI-assisted evaluation system.

The AI software independently evaluates digitized answers while faculty members simultaneously mark the scripts. The AI scores serve as a reference to assist the evaluator, but the faculty member's decision is always final.

Dual human-AI audit verification model.

💡 Pro Tip: Reach the exam centre early

Bengaluru traffic can be highly unpredictable. Plan to reach the examination centre well before the reporting time to avoid missing your exam.
