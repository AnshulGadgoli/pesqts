Graduation requisites require a cumulative total credit threshold. Failed modules receive 0 grade points, demanding either makeup exams or complete course repeats depending on cycle rules.

GPA Index Equation

\[SGPA = \frac{\sum (C_i \times G_i)}{\sum C_i}\]

Where \(C_i\) represents course credits and \(G_i\) is the grade point obtained.
