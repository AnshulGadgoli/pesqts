The Credit Structure

One of the first academic terms you'll hear at PESU is credits. Every course you complete carries a certain number of credits, and these credits, along with your grades, determine your academic progress and GPA.

To successfully complete your undergraduate programme, you'll need to earn 160 credits. Most courses are assigned one of the following credit values: 5, 4, or 2. Generally, lab courses carry higher credits, while other subjects may have lower credit values.

How Do You Earn Credits? You earn the entire credit value of a course only if you pass it by securing a grade between S and E. If you receive an F grade, you do not earn any credits for that course (it is an all-or-nothing system). You'll need to clear the course in a future semester to earn those credits.

GPA Index Equations

\[SGPA = \frac{\sum (C_i \times G_i)}{\sum C_i}\]

Where \(C_i\) represents course credits and \(G_i\) is the grade point obtained.

CGPA = Average of SGPA values

Where CGPA is calculated simply as the average of all your semester SGPA scores.

Marks & Grades Distribution

Each course is evaluated using both internal and external assessments. Weightage is split: Internal Assessment (50 marks) + End Semester Assessment (50 marks) = 100 Marks.

Internal Assessment Components (50M)

  • ISA 1: 20 Marks
  • ISA 2: 20 Marks
  • Assignments / Continuous Assessment: 10 Marks

External Assessment (50M)

The ESA is conducted for 100 marks, but the score is scaled down to 50 marks before being combined with your internal marks.

Final Marks = Internal (50) + Scaled ESA (50)

🧪 Lab-Integrated Courses

Some subjects are lab-integrated courses where both theory and laboratory components contribute. Marks: ISA + Assignments (Internal) 50, ESA (Theory) 100, Laboratory Component 20, Total Raw Marks 120.

Understanding Subject Codes

Every subject at PES University follows a structured subject code: UE JY SC Y C N A/B

• UE: Undergraduate Engineering
• JY: Year of Admission (e.g. 26 = 2026 Batch)
• SC: Subject Category (e.g., CS, EC, ME, CY, PH, MA)
• Y: Year of Study (1 = 1st Year, 2 = 2nd Year, etc.)
• C: Number of Credits
• N: Serial Number of the Subject within that credit category
• A / B: Semester Code (A = Odd Semester, B = Even Semester)
• Example: UE26CY151A represents: Undergraduate Eng., 2026 Batch, Chemistry, 1st Year, 5-credit, 1st subject, Odd Semester.
