Chapter V

Subjects in Each Cycle

"Two distinct pathways of physical and chemical science converging into a singular foundation."

CURRICULUM DEPARTMENTS
PHYSICS PATHWAY • CHEMISTRY PATHWAY

In the first year, students are divided into two academic cycles irrespective of their branch: the Physics Cycle and the Chemistry Cycle. This cycle determines your specific core technical subjects, while mathematics and programming courses are common to all.

Physics Cycle

Classical Engineering Track

Focused on mechanics, electrical fields, and environmental foundations.

  • Engineering Physics (with Laboratory) Credits: 5
  • Basic Mechanical Engineering Credits: 4
  • Basic Electrical Engineering Credits: 4
  • Environmental Studies (EVS) Credits: 2

Chemistry Cycle

Molecular Engineering Track

Focused on chemical processes, digital principles, and statistics.

  • Engineering Chemistry (with Laboratory) Credits: 5
  • Mechanical Statistics Credits: 4
  • Electronic Principles and Devices Credits: 4
  • Constitution of India Credits: 2

Common Subjects for Both Cycles

Mathematics I (M1)

Credits: 4

Core course syllabus: Differentiation, Differential Equations, and Alpha & Beta Functions.

Problem Solving with Python (with Lab)

Credits: 5

Hands-on programming logic, algorithmic scripts, data structures, and practical compile tasks in Python.

Mathematics II (M2)

Credits: 4

Core course syllabus: Integration, Probability models, and Laplace Transforms.

Problem Solving with C (with Lab)

Credits: 5

Low-level logic, memory pointers, structures, and C-compilation techniques.
