Before you dive into lectures, assignments, and campus life, here are a few essentials that every PESU student should know. Understanding daily timelines, registration requirements, and strict quotas sets you up for a smooth academic semester.

Class Timings

PES University follows two standard class schedules depending on the programme. Each lecture slot is 1 hour long, with a 30-minute short break and a 45-minute lunch break.

Slot Schedule A (B.Tech, BCA, MCA, Law, Design, Psych) Schedule B (Remaining Branches)

Slot 2 09:45 AM – 10:45 AM 09:00 AM – 10:00 AM
Short Break 10:45 AM – 11:15 AM 10:00 AM – 10:30 AM
Slot 3 11:15 AM – 12:15 PM 10:30 AM – 11:30 AM
Slot 4 12:15 PM – 01:15 PM 11:30 AM – 12:30 PM
Lunch Break 01:15 PM – 02:00 PM 12:30 PM – 01:15 PM

Timetable Swapping Rule: B.Tech, BCA, MCA, Law, Design, and Psychology follow Schedule B in the odd semester and Schedule A for the even semester. Other branches follow the opposite mapping (Schedule A in the odd semester and Schedule B in the even semester).

ID Card Policy

Your PESU ID card is compulsory for entering the campus and accessing various facilities. Forgot it? In some cases, showing your profile on the PESU App may work, but that's entirely at the discretion of the security staff. To avoid unnecessary delays, keep it with you at all times.

Attendance Quota

To be eligible for your End Semester Assessments (ESAs), you must maintain at least 75% attendance in every course. Attendance is calculated separately for each subject.

Relaxation for medical emergencies is evaluated on a case-by-case basis under strict documentation.

A Few Things to Remember

  • ⏰ Be on time: While some professors may allow late entry occasionally, attendance after the class has begun is entirely at their discretion.
  • 🚪 Grace Period: The first class of the day generally has a short grace period of about 5–10 minutes. This flexibility usually does not apply after breaks, so make sure you're back before the next session starts.
  • 📅 Timetable Shifts: Depending on your timetable, certain days may have fewer classes, lab sessions, or early finishes.
  • 🎉 Half-Days: Wednesdays are often scheduled as half-days for many programmes, although this can vary by department and semester.
